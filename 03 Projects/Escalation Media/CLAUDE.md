## Process

How work flows from start to delivered:

1. **Onboarding** (`00 Onboarding/`) — Client signed, briefed, set up in ClickUp
2. **Production** (`01 Production/`) — Content created (video shot, edited)
3. **Review** (`02 Review/`) — Internal QC, then client review and revisions
4. **Delivery** (`03 Delivery/`) — Final files sent, published, or handed off

## Where We're Headed

Long-term vision: evolve from a social media/content service into a full-service marketing 
agency with paid ads, stronger systems, and AI-assisted workflows (Omi + Obsidian + Claude). 
Building toward a team and systems that run without Ayden in every delivery decision.
